## Verdict
**Identified under stated assumptions — and the assumption doing all the work is not one
this graph can support.** Adjustment cannot identify this: the only backdoor path runs
through `departure_year_within_2026`, which *is* the assignment rule, so conditioning on it
destroys all treatment variation. DiD is the right family; parallel trends must be earned
empirically and one month of pre-period does not earn it. Full review with the nine
requirements: [[review]] (`wiki/questions/q-0001/review.md`).

## Events in this window
- [[2026-08-conversion-rate-flip-experiment]] — the treatment itself: the discount-tier /
  conversion-rate mapping inverted 2026-08-01 to 2026-08-14, applied to all members and
  routes but **only for departures within 2026**.
- Unnamed 2025 change — contaminates August 2025, so the year-on-year DiD is known biased.
  {source: raw/pwa_exp}
- **Nothing else.** The analyst confirmed that, as far as they know, no other change
  between July and September 2026 could move BA Holidays member bookings — no campaign, no
  BA Holidays pricing, inventory or site change. Recorded as a stated belief from one
  person, not an audit; the notebook should still test for unexplained level shifts either
  side of the window. {by:analyst on:2026-09-07}
- **Announcement timing still unknown.** The source never says whether members were told
  the flip was coming. Anticipation would show as a dip before 1 Aug and a spike after
  14 Aug, and that is testable in the notebook rather than worth another question.

## What the interview settled
- **Observability corrected.** All member bookings are visible, redeeming or not. Only
  *non-members* are hidden. This changed the question: the outcome is member booking
  volume, not just redeeming bookings. {by:analyst on:2026-09-07}
- **No pull-forward across the boundary.** 2027 departures are a different kind of trip
  with almost no overlap with near-term 2026 bookings, so the untreated group is not
  depleted by the treatment. The same answer is the parallel-trends threat.
  {by:analyst on:2026-09-07}
- **Assignment is a deterministic rule**: booking in 1–14 Aug 2026 **and** departure within
  2026. No member or route split. {source: raw/pwa_exp}
